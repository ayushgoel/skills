# North Star Documents

Use to present a project's future vision. Help readers understand the intended
experience or system, why it matters, and what becomes possible when it exists.
Choose the depth and narrative for the audience.

## Make The Vision Concrete

- Connect the future state to the problem or opportunity it addresses. Describe
  the outcomes readers should care about, using scenarios, examples, or visuals
  where they make the vision easier to understand.
- Explain how the major capabilities fit together and the boundaries that keep
  the vision coherent. Link to detailed designs and requirements rather than
  turning the vision into a full implementation specification.
- Distinguish present capability, committed work, and aspiration near the claims
  they qualify. A north star is not evidence of a delivery commitment.
- Show the important gaps, dependencies, or decisions between today and the
  target state. Include horizons or milestones when supported, without turning
  the page into an execution backlog.

## Connect Vision To Project Direction

- Respect the [project principles](principles-documents.md) and explain how the
  vision fits within them. Keep desired outcomes distinct from the non-variable
  constraints the project follows.
- Make unresolved alignments visible and support scale, cost, or benefit claims
  with evidence. Do not make the target state appear inevitable.
- Give readers a path to the owning decisions, designs, and work plans when
  they need to evaluate a choice or act on the vision.
